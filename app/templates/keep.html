Keep has no public API for personal accounts, so GKG signs in as an Android device would, with a master token. It is exchanged here from a short-lived cookie, stored only encrypted, and never shown again.

  1. Open accounts.google.com/EmbeddedSetup in a private window and sign in, 2-step verification included.
  2. Click I agree. The page may keep spinning; that is fine.
  3. Open DevTools (F12) › Application › Cookies › https://accounts.google.com and copy the value of oauth_token (it starts with oauth2_4/).
  4. Paste it below within a few minutes. It works once.

The master token opens the whole Google account, not only Keep. It does not expire by itself; to revoke it, remove the device from your Google account's devices.
